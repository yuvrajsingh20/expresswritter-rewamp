Orders & Delivery

Everything about placing, tracking, and receiving your orders.

How do I place an order?

▼

Browse all services, pick a variant, add any optional fast-track or +500 words add-ons, then proceed to checkout. You'll write your brief during checkout, pay securely, and a matching writer is assigned within 30 minutes (or 5 minutes for fast-track orders).

What's the typical turnaround time?

▼

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days for orders under 2,000 words. Fast-track (+50% surcharge) reduces this to 24–48 hours. Larger projects (thesis, books) follow a milestone-based schedule agreed during the brief.

Can I change my brief after ordering?

▼

Yes, until your writer accepts the order. Once accepted, small clarifications can be sent via in-app chat. Major scope changes may require a top-up payment — your writer will let you know upfront.

How will I know when my order is ready?

▼

You'll receive notifications via the platform, email, and (if enabled) WhatsApp. The status will move to Delivered on your dashboard, and you'll have 14 days to review before payment is auto-released.

Can I pick a specific writer?

▼

Yes — Pro members can request any writer they've worked with before from Dashboard → My Writers. Otherwise our matching algorithm assigns the best-fit writer based on subject expertise and current workload.

What file formats do you deliver in?

▼

By default: DOCX, PDF, and plain text. For LinkedIn or web content we also provide formatted HTML. Specify in your brief if you need anything else (Markdown, LaTeX, etc.).

Payments & Billing

Accepted methods, GST, invoices, and currency.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼

India: Razorpay handles UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major debit/credit cards, net-banking, and EMI on orders above ₹3,000.
International: Stripe for cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and SEPA. Crypto via partner gateway for orders above $200.

Is GST included?

▼

All listed prices are exclusive of 18% GST as per Indian tax law. The full breakdown (subtotal + platform fee + GST) is shown at checkout, and a GST-compliant invoice is auto-generated.

Can I get an invoice with my company name?

▼

Yes. Add your company name and GSTIN in Settings → Billing before placing the order. All future invoices will use these details and you can claim input tax credit.

Do you support multiple currencies?

▼

Pricing is shown in INR by default. We auto-detect your country and show converted prices in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, or CAD. The final charge is processed in your local currency by Stripe.

Refunds & Revisions

Our money-back guarantee in plain English.

What's your refund policy?

▼

Full refund if we can't match you with a writer, the deliverable is late, or it fails our plagiarism/AI standards. Partial refunds for cancellations after a writer is assigned. Full details in our Refund Policy.

How many revisions are included?

▼

Up to 3 free revisions within 14 days of delivery. After that, additional revisions are billed at the per-100-word rate of your service tier. Substantial scope changes are quoted separately.

How long do refunds take?

▼

Wallet credit: instant + 5% bonus. Original payment method: 5–7 business days depending on your bank. International cards may take up to 10 days.

Can I cancel after the writer started?

▼

Yes, but cancellation refunds are pro-rated. 70% before the first draft, 30% after first draft, and case-by-case after a full draft is submitted. The writer is compensated for work completed.

Account & Security

Login, password, MFA, and data.

How do I reset my password?

▼

Go to Sign in → "Forgot password" — enter your email and we'll send a reset link. The link expires in 1 hour.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

▼

In Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication. We support TOTP apps (Google Authenticator, Authy), SMS, and email codes. We strongly recommend an authenticator app over SMS.

How do I delete my account?

▼

Settings → Account → Delete account. We immediately suspend access and queue your data for deletion within 30 days (or longer where tax law requires retention). Active orders must be completed first.

Can I download all my data?

▼

Yes — Settings → Privacy → Export my data. You'll receive a ZIP with your profile, orders, messages, and invoices in JSON + DOCX formats within 24 hours.

What if I see a sign-in I didn't make?

▼

Immediately: Settings → Security → Active Sessions → "Sign out everywhere", then change your password. Contact [email protected] and we'll audit your account.

Quality & Plagiarism

How we guarantee originality and quality.

Do you check for plagiarism?

▼

Yes — every order is scanned with Turnitin (or Copyscape for non-academic work). We guarantee under 10% similarity. The full report is attached to your delivery email.

Do you use AI to write?

▼

Our writers are human. AI tools may be used as research assistants, but every deliverable is written by the assigned writer and scanned with GPTZero + Originality.ai to keep AI-detection scores under 15%.

How are writers vetted?

▼

4-step gauntlet: degree verification → portfolio review → 90-minute skill test (writing + grammar + subject-matter) → 3 supervised paid trial orders. Only 8% of applicants pass.

For Writers

Joining as a freelance writer, payouts, and policies.

How do I become a writer?

▼

Apply via our onboarding flow. You'll upload your portfolio, take a 90-min skill test, and complete 3 supervised trial orders before going live.

How much can I earn?

▼

Active writers earn ₹40,000–₹2,50,000/month depending on speciality and availability. Pay ranges from ₹0.80/word (entry) to ₹4.50/word (PhD/medical). Top earners take on 4–8 orders/week.

When are payouts processed?

▼

Weekly, every Monday — funds are released 7 days after order delivery (the client review window). Direct UPI/IMPS within India, Wise/PayPal internationally.

Can I choose which orders to take?

▼

Yes. Orders matching your speciality appear in your dashboard; you have 30 minutes to accept before they go to the next-best-fit writer. No penalty for declining.
